# Review Rubrics

## Shared rules

- Cite exact opaque evidence references, not vague concepts.
- Inspect neighboring text and the broader frozen corpus when the packet permits it.
- Separate answerability from relevance and citation correctness.
- Record uncertainty rather than forcing a grade.
- Escalate specialist-dependent claims.

## chunk_quality

Check coherent boundaries, sufficient context, primary/editorial separation, speaker or source attribution, section crossing, and whether the passage is usable as a retrieval unit.

## question_evidence

Check question clarity, corpus answerability, complete required evidence groups, missing or distracting evidence, cross-source coverage, and source attribution.

## source_scope_check

Check only conformance to the supplied approved scope policy. Do not decide whether a translation is authoritative or whether a work belongs in Late Antiquity.

## answer_claim

Check each atomic claim for full, partial, or absent support, citation alignment, overreach, contradiction, answer relevance, and appropriate refusal.

## Reason codes

Use enumerable reason codes where applicable:

- question_ambiguous
- answerability_dispute
- missing_evidence
- distractor_evidence
- relevance_grade_dispute
- cross_source_evidence_missing
- wrong_source_attribution
- citation_mismatch
- claim_overreach
- contradictory_evidence
- boundary_split
- insufficient_context
- primary_editorial_mix
- content_type_mismatch
- translation_or_edition_ambiguity
- historical_context_required
- candidate_pool_incomplete
- scope_policy_missing
- invalid_or_stale_input
